pftFactory ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") provides the NadeNade app ("the App"). This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in the App.

Summary

  • Names, memories, relationship information, and settings are generally stored only on your device.
  • The App does not require an account and does not display advertising or perform advertising-related tracking.
  • The App does not automatically send profile information to the Developer's servers.
  • In-app purchases use Apple services. Copies of profile information may be stored where you choose to transfer or back up your data.

1. Information Processed on Your Device

The App processes the following information on your device:

  • Optional names: the character's name and your name. These are used for display and the character's dialogue. You do not need to enter your legal name.
  • Usage and relationship records: petting counts, gentle-petting duration, last-used date, creation and update dates, memories and their dates, record identifiers, and viewed status. These are used to develop the relationship, vary reactions, and display and retain records.
  • Settings and display status: haptics, notifications, personality, completion of onboarding, and the character's daily mood and date. These are used to retain your selected settings and daily display.
  • Petting interaction information: touch locations and movements are processed on your device to determine the character's reactions and haptic feedback. Interaction traces are not sent to the Developer.

Profile information is not used for advertising or for tracking you by third parties.

2. Widget

To display the widget, the character's name, daily mood, and date are shared between the App and its widget on the same device through Apple's App Group feature. This information is not sent to the Developer's servers.

If you place the widget on your Home Screen, the character's name and other information may be visible to people who can see your device screen.

3. Camera and Notifications

Camera

The App requests camera access only when scanning a QR code to transfer your profile to another device. Scanning takes place on your device. The App does not save camera images or videos, and does not send them to the Developer.

You can use the core petting experience without granting camera access. You can change this permission in your device Settings.

Notifications

Only if you enable notifications, the App requests notification permission and schedules reminders on your device. These are not push notifications sent from the Developer's servers. Notifications may include the character's name.

You can turn off reminders in the App or in your device notification settings. Their appearance on the Lock Screen and elsewhere is controlled by your device settings.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchases and restoration of NadeNade Pro use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. The App uses product and transaction information provided by Apple to confirm purchases, provide paid features, restore purchases, and respond to refunds or revocations.

Product and transaction information obtained by the App is not sent to or stored on the Developer's servers.

Apple processes payments. The App does not obtain or store your Apple Account password or credit card number. The App does not require its own account, although an Apple Account may be required for purchases and restoration.

For information about Apple's handling of information, see App Store and Privacy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Transfers and Backups

On-device profile backup exclusion

The profile file stored by the App on your device is configured to be excluded from operating-system device backups, including iCloud Backup. This setting does not apply to backup files that you export yourself.

QR code transfers

When you display a QR code for transferring your profile, it includes profile information such as names, relationship progress, memories, and settings. On the receiving device, the profile is saved only after you confirm saving it.

QR codes are not encrypted. Compression and tamper detection do not keep their contents secret. Anyone who obtains the code may be able to read its contents. Please do not share or publish these codes, and manage any screenshots appropriately.

Password-protected backups

When you create a backup, the App encrypts your profile with the password you choose and exports it to a location you select. Passwords are not stored in your profile or backup file and are not sent to the Developer.

If you choose a cloud storage location such as iCloud Drive, the encrypted file may be stored or synchronized according to that service's settings. This is different from automatically synchronizing your profile to iCloud.

The Developer does not retain copies of your on-device profile or backups. If you lose a backup or forget its password, the Developer cannot restore the data.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Diagnostics

The App does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. It does not use advertising identifiers for tracking or track you across apps and websites.

Errors, including those related to haptics, may be recorded in operating-system diagnostic logs. The App does not automatically send those logs to the Developer.

Diagnostic and usage information handled by Apple under your device settings and Apple's policies is separate from the App's handling of information. The Developer may use aggregate statistics and crash reports made available by Apple to investigate and improve issues.

7. Retention and Deletion

Your on-device profile remains stored until you reset it. To delete it, use Settings → Data Management → Reset This Device's Data in the App.

Resetting restores names, relationship information, memories, and settings on that device to their initial state. It also resets widget information and scheduled reminders. Consider creating a backup before resetting data you wish to retain.

Resetting does not delete the following:

  • Profiles transferred to another device. Reset data separately on that device if necessary.
  • Exported backup files, QR code screenshots, and copies stored in cloud storage. Delete these separately in the relevant service or in Files or Photos.
  • Purchase history and Pro purchase rights managed by Apple. Use Apple services for purchase-related requests.

Restoring a backup stores its included information on your device again. The Developer cannot remotely access or delete data stored on your device.

8. Information Included in Inquiries

If you contact the Developer, we receive your contact information, inquiry content, and any materials you choose to attach. This information is used to respond to your inquiry, investigate issues, and take necessary action.

We do not set a uniform retention period for inquiry information. We delete it after responding to the inquiry, investigating issues, and completing necessary action, once it is no longer necessary to retain it. This does not apply where retention is required by law.

Please do not send legal names, passwords, payment information, transfer QR codes, backup files, or other sensitive information unnecessarily.

To request confirmation, correction, or deletion of information provided through an inquiry, contact us using the details below. We will confirm your identity and respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. External Services and Third-Party Sharing

The Developer does not sell your information or provide it to third parties for advertising purposes. Information received through inquiries is not provided to third parties except with your consent or where required by law.

The Developer does not outsource the handling of inquiry information to external service providers.

Apple purchase processing, backup storage locations you select, and services used to send and receive email are governed by the respective services' privacy policies.
